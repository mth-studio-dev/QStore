Privacy Policy

QStore respects your privacy. Camera access is used strictly on-device to scan QR codes and no photos or videos are uploaded. If you use Guest Mode, your QR codes stay only on your device. If you choose to sign in with Google, your email address, user ID, and saved QR codes are stored securely in Firebase solely to sync your library across your devices.
